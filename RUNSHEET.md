# AURAMAXX — RUNSHEET, the 3-minute demo

Pitch order = submission order. Slides optional; the demo is the point. Voting is 50% judges /
50% the room (to be confirmed), and the room votes on their phones **while you talk** — so the
app must be in their hands before you open your mouth.

**Before you are called:** QR already on the projector / printed table cards out since mid-
afternoon, 25+ people already playing with a score they care about. `/op` in B's hands. Laptop on
hotspot. `Win+P` → Extend. Chrome fullscreen on display 2. Camera framed and threshold tuned.

---

### 0:00 – 0:30 · the hook (a live round, no preamble)

The board is already live behind you when you walk up.

> **"Phones out, scan the screen — you've each got a thousand free AURA and fifteen seconds. Odd, or even?"**

15-second countdown, huge. Two buttons on every phone. **Then say nothing for eight seconds.**
Odds swing, pot climbs, names fly onto the tape.

At the lock: an audible CLUNK. One beat. **"…Seven."** Hit resolve.

**The payout rain.** Every name flashes green or red at once:
`1 TRANSACTION · 61 PAID · 0.5 SECONDS · 0x8f3a…`

> **"Sixty-one of you just got paid. By one transaction. In half a second."**
> **"Your bank moves money to one person in three days."**

### 0:30 – 1:00 · what it is, and why you cannot cheat me

> "Everyone's AURA go in one pot. The pot splits between whoever was right. That's it — that's the
> tote board, invented in Paris."
> "Which means I take no position and I cannot win this. And I am the only person in this room
> who is physically blocked from betting — three lines in the contract, verified, on screen."

Point at the board: **"Those odds aren't a chart I drew. They're the pool balances, on chain, repricing every 302 milliseconds."**

*(Optional, only if running early: "grey tick means the network has seen your bet, solid means it
can never be undone. Look at your phone.")*

### 1:00 – 2:10 · the camera round

Live video full bleed — the room sees itself.

> "Look up. That's you. There's a button on your phone that says LIGHT UP. Brightness all the way
> up. **Don't press it yet.**"
> "More than half of you, or not. The line is half of whoever bets — **the contract works that
> out, I don't touch it.** Fifteen seconds."

**LOCK.** → **"Bets are locked. You cannot change your bet."**

*Only now:* **"Lights up — and you're allowed to help your side."**

Twenty seconds. Count climbing at 320 px, backend tally beside it. **Say nothing.**

`FINAL 43 · LINE 37 · OVER` → resolve → second payout rain.

> **"You didn't predict that number. You bought it. That's an oracle attack, you all just did one,
> and it took you nine seconds."**

### 2:10 – 2:45 · the numbers, all recomputable from the screen

> "Tonight: N transactions. The biggest settlement was one transaction paying M people in X
> milliseconds — that hash is on the screen, open it on the phone you're holding."
> "Eighty people tapping is about four transactions a second. That is not a throughput story and
> I won't pretend it is. What this chain gives me is one transaction big enough to pay the whole
> room, landing in a third of a second — the difference between a game show and a queue."

### 2:45 – 3:00 · close

The leaderboard, full screen, with the top name huge.

> **"«Name» finishes top of the room tonight."** — DVB hands over the swag, live, on stage.
> **"Everyone here has a score. Nobody installed anything, nobody has a wallet, nobody spent a
> cent — and every one of those bets was a signature you made yourself."**

## Lines to have ready

**If the two counts differ on screen:** *"The camera counted 43, my server knows 41 phones tapped.
Within two — and I'd never settle real money on this, which is exactly the point of that round."*

**Before the camera round, unprompted, French then English:** *« Cette caméra est la mienne, elle
filme cette salle, rien n'est enregistré, rien ne quitte cet ordinateur — seul un chiffre est
envoyé. »*

**If the room does not play along:** one shout — *"UNDER, what are you doing?"* That is compering,
not cheating. The line is 45% of *registered players*, so it scales with the room, and UNDER winning by suppression
is loud and funny.

**If the RPC queues:** *"Your bets are all signed and waiting — watch them land at once."* The
flush is spectacular and reads as competence.

**If another team already demoed a betting app:** *"Theirs lets you place a bet. Mine pays the
whole room at once."*

## Never say

- Any TPS number.
- "Trustless", or that the camera was exact.
- Anything about traffic-cam casino games.
- A block number, hash or count that is not real.
